# Risk Scoring

Each open finding contributes points:

- Critical: 25
- High: 15
- Medium: 8
- Low: 3
- Info: 1

The project score is capped at 100.

Risk levels:

- 0-20: Low
- 21-50: Medium
- 51-75: High
- 76-100: Critical

Findings should include title, severity, category, affected asset, evidence, business impact, recommendation, status, first seen, and last seen.
